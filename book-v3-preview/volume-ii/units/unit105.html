105 极限加法的估计：两个 ε/2 界相加

Unit 105 — Adding Two ε/2 Bounds

6 个定理 · 6 道练习 · 18 条提示 · 解答附录

打印 / PDF 本单元

两个收敛序列逐点相加，极限也相加。这条结论的全部算术都在一个式子里：把 (x + y) - ((L + M) + eps) 拆成两段各 eps/2 的差。拆开的每一步都要和 ratAddRep 的括号、次序打交道，直接塞进极限的证明会让量词和代数混在一起。

105.0 减法对第二个因子同余

练习 1ratSubRep_wd_snd定理附录解答

105.0–105.1 先备两条搬运律：减法对第二个因子同余，四个加数重新配对。105.2–105.3 把两条差界相加，并把两个误差对齐到同一个 eps；105.4 把 d/2 的两条界收成一条 d 界。105.5 是一个具体实例。

边界：只做代表元层的估计，不声称任何序列收敛，也不在类层谈加法。不新增公理。

x - y 展开是 x + (-y)。要把它换成 x - y'，只需要取负保持等价，再用加法对第二个因子的同余。

ratSubRep 就是 ratAddRep x (ratNegRep y)；先用 ratNegRep_wd 把 y ~ y' 搬到负元上，再交给加法第二个因子的同余。

ratRel y y' → ratRel (x - y) (x - y')。

105.1 四个加数重新配对

练习 2ratAddRep_shift定理附录解答

(a + g) + (b + g) 与 (a + b) + (g + g) 只差括号与中间两项的次序。先把外层的结合律拆开，把 g + (b + g) 重排成 b + (g + g)，最后再把 a + (b + (g + g)) 收回成 (a + b) + (g + g)。

theorem ratAddRep_shift (a b g : ratRep) :
    ratRel (ratAddRep (ratAddRep a g) (ratAddRep b g))
           (ratAddRep (ratAddRep a b) (ratAddRep g g)) :=
  sorry
提示 1思路

三段链。第一段用 ratAdd_assoc a g (b+g) 拆外层；第二段在 a + … 的第二项上用结合律、交换律把 g + (b+g) 换成 b + (g+g)；第三段用结合律的对称收回。

提示 2目标形态

(a + g) + (b + g) ~ (a + b) + (g + g)。

提示 3关键件

ratAdd_assoc（正反两次）· ratAdd_comm · ratAddRep_wd_gen · ratAddRep_wd_snd_gen · ratRel_trans_gen · ratRel_symm。

theorem ratAddRep_shift (a b g : ratRep) :
    ratRel (ratAddRep (ratAddRep a g) (ratAddRep b g))
           (ratAddRep (ratAddRep a b) (ratAddRep g g)) :=
  ratRel_trans_gen
    (ratAddRep (ratAddRep a g) (ratAddRep b g))
    (ratAddRep a (ratAddRep g (ratAddRep b g)))
    (ratAddRep (ratAddRep a b) (ratAddRep g g))
    (ratAdd_assoc a g (ratAddRep b g))
    (ratRel_trans_gen
      (ratAddRep a (ratAddRep g (ratAddRep b g)))
      (ratAddRep a (ratAddRep b (ratAddRep g g)))
      (ratAddRep (ratAddRep a b) (ratAddRep g g))
      (ratAddRep_wd_snd_gen a
        (ratAddRep g (ratAddRep b g))
        (ratAddRep b (ratAddRep g g))
        (ratRel_trans_gen
          (ratAddRep g (ratAddRep b g))
          (ratAddRep (ratAddRep g b) g)
          (ratAddRep b (ratAddRep g g))
          (ratRel_symm (ratAddRep (ratAddRep g b) g)
            (ratAddRep g (ratAddRep b g))
            (ratAdd_assoc g b g))
          (ratRel_trans_gen
            (ratAddRep (ratAddRep g b) g)
            (ratAddRep (ratAddRep b g) g)
            (ratAddRep b (ratAddRep g g))
            (ratAddRep_wd_gen (ratAddRep g b) (ratAddRep b g) g
              (ratAdd_comm g b))
            (ratAdd_assoc b g g))))
      (ratRel_symm
        (ratAddRep (ratAddRep a b) (ratAddRep g g))
        (ratAddRep a (ratAddRep b (ratAddRep g g)))
        (ratAdd_assoc a b (ratAddRep g g))))
